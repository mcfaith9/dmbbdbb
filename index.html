How to Add Your Own Pictures

  1. Put your party photos inside the images/ folder next to index.html.
  2. Open script.js in any text editor and update the filenames inside GAME_CONFIG.images.
  3. Save and click Restart Game. Each picture is automatically duplicated into a matching pair and shuffled.
